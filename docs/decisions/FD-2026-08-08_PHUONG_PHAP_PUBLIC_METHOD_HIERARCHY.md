## 1. Current ruling

ESSENCE retains this six-point map as its **internal orientation architecture**:

> LẶNG → AN ĐỊNH → NHẬN RA NHỊP RIÊNG → NHÌN RA BẢN SẮC → CHỌN MỘT NHỊP SỐNG CÓ THỂ GIỮ → AN THỊNH

`/phuong-phap` must **not** publish that map as an independent framework, second framework, public diagram, six-step sequence, progress system, card system, level system or explanatory comparison.

The only public Method hierarchy on `/phuong-phap` is:

> 01 — Chill với cảm xúc
>
> 02 — Thách thức giới hạn
>
> 03 — Hiện thực ước mơ

The meanings of the internal map are integrated into those three public movements:

- **Chill** carries the spirit of Lặng and the foundation of An Định.
- **Thách thức** carries the work of noticing rhythm, roles and patterns, what remains appropriate, and the capacity to choose differently.
- **Hiện thực** carries the work of turning recognition into a sustainable rhythm of life and moving toward a life one actively creates.

`An Thịnh` may appear naturally in prose exactly as supplied by the approved page copy. It must not become a separate public heading, card, badge, diagram, level or sub-framework.
